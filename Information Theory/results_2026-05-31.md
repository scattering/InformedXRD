### Important interpretation correction for bits_over_prior

The lower-bound derivation is valid:
- `bits_over_prior = E[log2 q(Y|X)] - E[log2 p(Y)]`
- with `p` set to the true test marginal, this is a lower bound on `I(Y;X)`

But the original “zero-centered null” intuition was wrong for a sharp model.

Permutation null:
- not centered at `0`
- centered at the model’s own average sharpening away from the prior
- here null mean is `-0.6602 bits`

Observed vs null:
- observed `-0.6099`
- null mean `-0.6602`
- excess over null mean: about `+0.0503 bits`

So:
- this is **not** a flat-prior signature
- but it is also **not** a clean positive lower-bound result
- the signal is marginal and calibration-sensitive
